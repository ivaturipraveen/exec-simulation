## Summary

Our first Dual Eligible Special Needs Plan (D-SNP) went live on 1 January 2026 in six counties in Texas and Florida. We enrolled **3,100 members in four months**, against a first-year plan of 2,200. About 2,700 are in new counties and 400 are in legacy counties. Growth came mostly from the quarterly dual special enrollment period and from broker channels in Pecos Ridge, Sabine Crossing and Suwannee Bend counties.

The Model of Care (MOC) approved by CMS commits us to a health risk assessment (HRA) within 90 days of enrollment, an individualized care plan (ICP) for every member, and an interdisciplinary care team (ICT) for members at higher risk. Care model staffing in the approved budget is listed as **"to follow."** It has not been funded.

## What we are seeing

- **Contact is hard.** HRA outreach started by text and app invitation, then moved to phone. 27% of D-SNP members have a phone number that fails or reaches someone else. Mailing addresses changed for 9% of members in the first 90 days.
- **Loss of Medicaid eligibility.** 112 members lost Medicaid eligibility in the first four months and moved into the six-month deeming period. Tracking is manual.
- **The care management team is stretched.** Horizon has 22 care managers plan-wide, and their caseloads already average 118 active cases. Three outreach coordinators were moved to D-SNP HRA calls in March, which paused other outreach.

## Exposure

The MOC is scored at CMS audit, and D-SNP members count in all of our Star measures. Dual-eligible members historically respond less to digital reminders and more to pharmacist or care manager calls. A program audit finding on HRA timeliness would also show up in our compliance record.

## Request

1. Fund three dedicated D-SNP care managers and one social worker (estimated $0.52M per year fully loaded). This is not in the Round 1 transformation budget.
2. Name an executive owner for MOC compliance. The role is currently shared between Clinical and Compliance.
3. Decide whether the 2027 bid expands the D-SNP to the remaining three new counties before the care model is staffed.
