# Employed Clinician Compensation Summary, FY2026

**From:** Patricia Wynn, Chief Human Resources Officer, Cascade Valley Health
**To:** Dana Whitcomb, President and CEO, CommunityCare Alliance; Dr. Daniel Cho, VP Primary Care, Cascade Valley Health
**Date:** 11 June 2026
**Re:** Information request from the plan on primary care compensation design

As requested for the plan's strategy review, here is a summary of how Cascade Valley Health (CVH) pays its employed primary care physicians (PCPs). Individual compensation data is confidential and is not included.

## Population

- **210 employed PCPs** (184 physicians, 26 nurse practitioners and physician assistants with their own panels) across 22 primary care clinics.
- Median panel: 1,880 patients across all payers, of whom about 370 are CommunityCare Alliance members. Plan members are about 20% of the average employed PCP's panel. The largest other payers are traditional Medicare (24%) and commercial plans (38%).

## FY2026 compensation model (physicians)

| Component | Basis | Share of median total cash |
|---|---|---|
| Base salary | Market-referenced salary by specialty and years of service; median $268,000 | 88% |
| Productivity bonus | Work relative value units (wRVUs) above 4,900 a year, paid at $46 per wRVU, quarterly | 9% |
| Citizenship component | Committee service, teaching and chart closure within 72 hours | 3% |
| **Quality or Star Ratings component** | **None** | **0%** |

- The productivity bonus is earned by 57% of employed physicians. Visits drive wRVUs. Non-visit work (portal messages, outreach, care-gap review, refill renewals) earns no wRVUs.
- The system's 2023 compensation redesign considered a 5% "quality pool" tied to all-payer measures. The system compensation committee deferred it because "measures differ by payer and would create administrative burden." It has not been revisited.
- Advanced practice clinicians are salaried with no productivity or quality component.

## Comparison: independent network PCPs

The plan's separate contracts with its 90 independent PCPs include a **Quality Partnership addendum** worth up to $4 per member per month (PMPM), tied to 6 Stars measures. CVH employed PCPs are not party to plan provider contracts. Their services are covered under the plan-system services agreement, which pays the system on a capitation basis with no measure-linked terms.

## Clinic director goals

Clinic directors' FY2026 goals are access (new-patient visits), wRVU productivity, patient experience on the system's own all-payer survey, and budget. Plan Star Ratings measures do not appear in clinic director goals or in the system's primary care dashboard.

## Considerations for any change

- Any compensation change for employed physicians requires approval by the CVH Physician Compensation Committee, which meets in March and September. Changes take effect at the start of the fiscal year (1 July).
- A payer-specific incentive would need legal review under the system's fair-market-value and compensation-plan rules, typically 8 to 12 weeks.
- Physician leaders have told HR that "more measures without time" will not be well received. The 2025 engagement survey ranked "administrative burden" as the top source of burnout (64%).
