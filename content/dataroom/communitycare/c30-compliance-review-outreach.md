# Compliance Review: Member Outreach Volume and Complaint Exposure

**Source:** Internal audit, CommunityCare Alliance Compliance Department
**Audit period:** 1 October 2025 to 30 June 2026
**Owner:** Hannah Feld, Chief Compliance Officer; lead auditor Victor Ramos, Senior Compliance Auditor
**Report date:** 21 August 2026
**Distribution:** Plan Compliance Committee; Audit Committee of the Plan board; VP Stars and Quality; VP Member Experience

## Scope

The audit assessed whether member outreach campaigns (screening, wellness, adherence and gap closure) comply with CMS communications and marketing guidelines and with Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) consent rules. It also assessed whether outreach practices create complaint and CAHPS exposure.

## Method

- Reviewed 9 campaign designs and 2,400 sampled outreach records from the CareReach platform.
- Matched outreach records to Member Services CRM contacts, grievances, Complaints Tracking Module (CTM) cases and shared EHR scheduling data.
- Interviewed the Engagement, Quality and Member Services teams.

## Findings

| # | Finding | Rating |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Communications content and disclaimers met CMS requirements in 98% of sampled items. | Low |
| 2 | Consent records for SMS were complete for 97% of sampled members. 41 members were texted after opting out, because a platform sync failed in January 2026. | Medium |
| 3 | **Outreach volume is not linked to appointment capacity.** Campaigns ask members to book visits and mammograms without checking slot availability. In the sample, the median wait to the first available slot after outreach was 44 days for mammography and 24 days for PCP visits. | **High** |
| 4 | Members contacted 3 or more times for the same gap, with no appointment available within 30 days, were **3.4 times as likely** to file a grievance or CTM complaint as members contacted once. | **High** |
| 5 | Complaints linked to outreach ("told to schedule, no slot available") rose from 1 a month in Q4 2025 to 5 a month in Q2 2026. | Medium |
| 6 | Campaign success is measured by reach (contacts completed). No campaign measures completed appointments, complaints or opt-outs. | Medium |

## Exposure assessment

- Complaints about the Health Plan (M11) is at 0.20 per 1,000 members, inside the 4-Star band (0.10 to 0.24). Access-related complaints are 58% of the total.
- If outreach volume grows by the 30% proposed in the 2027 engagement plan without more appointment capacity, Compliance projects the complaint rate could reach **0.25 to 0.28 per 1,000**, which is 3-Star territory.
- Outreach that cannot be fulfilled may also depress CAHPS Getting Appointments and Care Quickly responses. Members surveyed in spring are asked about the prior 6 months.

## Recommendations

1. Before a campaign goes live, set a frequency cap: no more than 2 contacts for the same gap in 60 days.
2. Before a campaign launches, check appointment and imaging slot availability with clinic operations.
3. Add complaint, opt-out and completed-appointment metrics to campaign scorecards.
4. Fix the SMS opt-out sync and remediate the 41 affected members (completed 14 August 2026).

## Management response

*Engagement team (draft):* Agrees with recommendations 3 and 4. On recommendations 1 and 2, the team notes that outreach reach drives "member awareness". It asks that frequency caps be deferred until after the 2027 breast screening wave.

*Compliance:* Findings 3 and 4 remain open and are rated high. They will be reported to the Audit Committee in October 2026.
