## Context

- The company is backed by private equity (Ridgeline Capital Partners). The next funding round is planned for late 2027.
- Board mandate: **4.0 Stars within two rating cycles, without slowing county expansion.** Two to four more counties are under evaluation for 2028.
- Crossing 4.0 Stars is worth about $31M per year in quality bonus payments (see H-26).

## What the board expects

1. **A visible Stars signal within 12 months.** The board has been explicit: it wants evidence that measures are moving, not only that programs have started. Leading indicators tied to specific measures will count, if we can show the link.
2. **No "two-year foundation program."** The board is comfortable with speed and capital and uncomfortable with long infrastructure projects that show nothing by the next review. A foundation investment needs a clear early milestone.
3. **Responsible AI.** Leadership and the board are enthusiastic about AI. After two outreach near-misses this year (see the privacy log), the Audit and Risk Committee asked that any member-facing automation include validation and a kill switch.
4. **Growth continues.** Investments must work for members enrolling in 2027 as well as current members.

## Planning guidance

- Every proposal should state its cost, its capacity points per quarter, the measures and leading indicators it moves, its prerequisites, and its risks.
- Operating costs that continue after Round 1 (stewardship, incentive pools, staffing) must be identified. Recurring costs above $3M per year need board approval.
- Proposals must not double-count benefits. The finance team will reconcile any benefit figure to a data-room source.
- Capacity is the binding constraint more often than cash. Last year, six of nine quality initiatives were abandoned mid-year.
