## Commentary

1. **The rating has been 3.5 for three cycles.** Clinical outcome measures (M3, M4) and screening (M5) have held at 4 Stars. Both adherence measures (M1, M2) sit 1.0 point below the 4-Star line and have improved by about 0.2 points a year.
2. **Getting Appointments and Care Quickly (M9) is the only measure at 2 Stars,** and it has declined in each cycle (73.6, then 72.9, then 72.0). It carries a weight of 2.
3. **Customer Service (M10) is at 4 Stars** and rising. Complaints (M11) are at 4 Stars but have edged up by 0.01 in each cycle.
4. **Readmissions (M7)** improved by 0.3 over two cycles, and the plan remains 1.1 above the 4-Star threshold.
5. Breast screening dipped by 0.4 points in the 2026 cycle, the first decline since the plan launched.

## Notes

- The 2026 Star Ratings reflect measurement year 2024 for most HEDIS measures and the spring 2025 CAHPS survey.
- No measure was affected by a CMS data integrity reduction or by disaster-related adjustments in any cycle.
