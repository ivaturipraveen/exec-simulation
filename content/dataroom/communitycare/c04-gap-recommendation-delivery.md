## Commentary

- **Delivery works.** In every month, 100% of recommendations were posted to the portal on schedule, and e-mail notifications went out on the first business day.
- **Recommendation quality is high.** Clinical reviewers judged 88.5% of recommendations appropriate, and when a recommendation was opened, a third were acted on.
- **Engagement is the gap.** The open rate fell from 6.8% to 5.5% over the year. Most opens happen when a clinic medical assistant downloads the monthly PDF.
- Independent PCPs open portal items at more than twice the employed-PCP rate.
- Analytics recommends a refresher training campaign and a monthly leaderboard by clinic, which the team can deliver in Q4 2026 at an estimated cost of $85,000.

*Definitions:* "Opened" means the item was viewed in the portal or its PDF was downloaded. "Acted on" means a matching order, referral, prescription change or visit was recorded in the EHR within 60 days, whether or not the portal was opened. A 2.0% acted-on rate therefore includes some actions taken without the recommendation.
