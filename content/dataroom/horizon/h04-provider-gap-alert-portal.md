## Link to the measure model

The provider alert action rate is a leading indicator for M3 (Controlling Blood Pressure) and M4 (Glycemic Status Assessment). Supplemental data submissions averaged 96 per month, and 81% of them came from the two risk-bearing groups. Of the 14,900 blood pressure alerts, 8,700 (58%) were "no reading on file" rather than "reading above 140/90."

## Commentary

Delivery is not the problem: 99.6% of alerts were delivered to a registered practice inbox. Opening and action are concentrated in two groups. Practice login failures generated 214 help-desk tickets in the period, and 63% came from new-county practices (single sign-on setup was never completed for 140 practices onboarded in 2025 and 2026). Network Management has asked for a second channel (fax or EHR inbox), but no budget is assigned.
