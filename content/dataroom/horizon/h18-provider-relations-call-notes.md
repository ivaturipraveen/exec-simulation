## Representative summary (August 2026)

- 12 practice contacts logged; 9 practices visited in person; 3 representatives for 268 new-county practices.
- Recurring themes: practices do not recognize Horizon; portal access problems; no payment for reporting data; attribution lists include patients the practice has not seen.
- Where a practice received a simple gap list by fax or email, it closed some gaps quickly, as Pineywoods did.
- Network Management has requested a fourth representative and a fax channel for gap alerts. Neither is funded.
