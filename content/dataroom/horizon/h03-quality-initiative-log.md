## Program manager comment

Most abandonments happened when a person was pulled onto growth work: new-county launches, the D-SNP launch, or annual enrollment. We have never run a retrospective. Four initiatives were approved in a staff meeting without a named owner, and the tracker was the only record. For 2027 planning, the team suggests each initiative needs a named executive owner, a success metric, and a protected resource before launch.
