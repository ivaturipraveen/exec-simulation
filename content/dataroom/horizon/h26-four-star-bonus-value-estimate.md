# 4-Star Quality Bonus Value Estimate

**From:** Finance and Actuarial (Monique Tran, Director, Revenue Analytics)
**To:** Tom Rasmussen, Chief Financial Officer; Executive leadership team
**Date:** 28 August 2026
**Classification:** Illustrative planning estimate for the transformation program

## Bottom line

Crossing from 3.0 to **4.0 Stars is worth about $31M per year** at current membership. The estimate uses an illustrative quality bonus and rebate value of **$600 per member per year (PMPY)**.

## Calculation

| Input | Value |
|---|---|
| Current membership (August 2026) | 52,400 |
| Illustrative value of reaching 4.0 Stars | $600 PMPY |
| Annual value at current membership | 52,400 × $600 = **$31.4M** (rounded to $31M) |

## Sensitivity

| Scenario | Members | Annual value at $600 PMPY |
|---|---|---|
| Membership flat | 52,400 | $31.4M |
| Continued growth (+15% per year) | 60,300 | $36.2M |
| Expansion paused | 52,400 | $31.4M |
| Membership falls 10% (complaints and disenrollment) | 47,200 | $28.3M |

## What does not count

- **3.5 Stars is not 4.0.** In this planning model, the bonus value applies only at 4.0 Stars or higher. Reaching 3.5 Stars brings some rebate and marketing benefit, but we have not valued it here. The board should not treat 3.5 as partial bonus credit.
- **Timing.** By game convention, the bonus is paid in the year after the rating is earned. A rating earned at the end of the second cycle pays from the following year. Nothing in the Round 1 budget is offset by bonus revenue.
- **Cost avoidance is separate.** Lower readmissions or fewer complaints may bring savings, but they are not part of this figure. Proposals that claim them must show their own source.

## Comparison with program costs

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Round 1 transformation capital | $18.0M |
| Round 2 capital (base plus maximum earned) | $8.0M + up to $4.0M |
| Annual bonus value at 4.0 Stars | $31M |

At this value, one year at 4.0 Stars would recover the full Round 1 commitment. The value exists only if the rating crosses the threshold. Spending that leaves the rating at 3.5 Stars returns little on this measure.

## Caveats

This is an illustrative estimate for executive education. It is not a CMS payment calculation and does not include benchmark, county rate or rebate-percentage mechanics.
