## Summary

Heritage holds clinical data-sharing agreements with **2 of the 5 major health systems** in its service area. Agreements with the other **3 have been pending for 14 months**. All three are drafted, and none is blocked on a legal point of substance. Each is waiting on a named Heritage business owner to sign the data-use schedule and fund the interface work.

## Notes on the pending agreements

- **Brightwater** has accepted our privacy terms. Its last open question (March 2026) was "who on the Heritage side owns the use of the data and the follow-up workflow?" We have not answered it.
- **Cobalt Bay** sent a signature-ready version in February 2026. Legal approved it, but the agreement needs an executive signatory at VP level or above, and no business owner has been assigned since the Lakeshore integration lead left in March 2024.
- **Sablewood** requires the plan to fund its interface engine work, estimated at $140,000. The request was not included in the 2026 operating budget.
- The health systems' view, as summarized by their privacy officers, is that they are "ready when Heritage is."
