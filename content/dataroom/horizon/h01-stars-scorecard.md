## Commentary

- **What is working.** Readmissions (M7) and appointment access (M9) are at 4 Stars. The membership is younger than the market (average age 71.2), which helps both.
- **What is stuck.** Blood pressure control (M3) and complaints (M11) have been at 2 Stars for all three cycles. M3 is a triple-weighted measure, so it costs more than any other single gap.
- **What slipped.** Breast cancer screening fell 4.3 points across two cycles and dropped from 3 to 2 Stars in 2026. The 27-month lookback means members who joined recently have had less time to complete a mammogram on our book.
- **Adherence.** M1 and M2 sit mid-band at 3 Stars. M1 needs 2.0 points to reach the 4-Star threshold of 86.0; M2 needs 1.5 points to reach 88.0.
- **Two-year headroom** (from the planning model): M1 +4.5, M2 +3.5, M3 +12, M4 +6, M5 +10, M7 -1.0, M9 +2, M10 +4, M11 -0.30, M12 +5.

The board mandate is 4.0 Stars within two rating cycles. At current values, no single measure gets us there; at least four triple-weighted measures need to move a full Star band.
