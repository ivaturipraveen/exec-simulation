# Star Ratings in two pages — optional pre-read
_Simulated, fictional training material. Stars values are illustrative and not predictive._

## Page 1 · How the rating works
Medicare Advantage plans are rated from 1 to 5 Stars on quality, member experience and access. In this simulation a plan's summary rating is the weighted average of the measures below, rounded to the nearest half Star.

**Why it matters.** Crossing 4 Stars unlocks a quality bonus (illustrative $600 per member per year here). Falling below it costs the bonus and the story you tell your board.

**Weights.** Outcome and intermediate-outcome measures count most; member experience and complaints/access next; process measures least.

**Measures lag.** Leading indicators (call abandonment, gap-closure rate, outreach reach) move within quarters. Measures move in the next rating year, so most of a Year 1 investment shows up in Year 2.
- Leading KPIs move within 1 quarter of ADT feed go-live; measure moves next rating year at 50% of full effect
- Leading KPIs move within 1 quarter of go-live; measure moves in the next rating year at 50% of full effect, full effect the year after
- Leading KPIs move within 1 quarter; measure moves next rating year at 60% of full effect
- Leading KPIs move within 1 quarter; measure moves next rating year at 70% of full effect

## Page 2 · How the simulation turns decisions into results
- **Fit matters.** A card's effect depends on your plan: high fit 100%, medium 60%, low 30% of its base effect.
- **Diminishing returns.** The 1st, 2nd and 3rd card on the same measure deliver 100%, 60%, 30% of their effect.
- **Adoption.** Copilots and agents land at 60% with workflow redesign and 85% with incentives as well.
- **Capacity.** Every card uses implementation capacity. Going over slows everything (up to 1.5× your supply).
- **Prerequisites.** Some cards need a foundation first; without it they deliver little.
- **Crises follow choices.** What you fund (and skip) decides which events can fire.

3. What will we see in the first two quarters that tells us it is working?
