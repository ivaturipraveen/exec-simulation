# Crisis packets (read to the team)
_Simulated, fictional training material. Stars values are illustrative and not predictive._

## E1 Eligibility assumption drives inappropriate outreach to 11,400 members
*Applies to:* Horizon Health · *Time box:* 15 minutes

> Monday 8:10 am. The engagement platform sent a 'your diabetes medication refill is overdue' message to 11,400 members over the weekend. Early analysis: a segmentation rule used a prior-plan eligibility flag that was never validated for new-county members. At least 2,900 recipients have no diabetes diagnosis. The contact center has 1,100 calls in queue. A member's daughter posted the message on social media; a local TV station has called. Nobody is sure whether the platform is still sending. You have 15 minutes.

**Leadership test:** Speed versus containment; member communication; validation; kill switch; accountability.

---

## E2 Acquired-plan member data cannot be reconciled; flagship initiative slips six months
*Applies to:* Heritage Medicare · *Time box:* 15 minutes

> Quarterly review prep, two days out. The data team reports that the Lakeshore identity crosswalk fails validation: 19% of matches are wrong, including 1,400 members matched to deceased records. The predictive adherence model has been scoring the wrong people for two months. Outreach lists for Q3 are frozen. The CIO proposes a six-month identity remediation; the CFO wants to know what the board hears on Thursday. The Lakeshore contract loses a quarter of adherence outreach. You have 15 minutes.

**Leadership test:** Scope, sequencing, data ownership, salvage path, board disclosure.

---

## E3 Clinically sound recommendations are ignored by providers
*Applies to:* CommunityCare Alliance · *Time box:* 15 minutes

> The system CMIO has asked for a meeting. The provider copilot has generated 41,000 recommendations in two quarters; clinicians have acted on 2,900. A clinic director survey calls it 'another plan portal'. Three clinics have disabled alerts. The system board meets in two weeks and the plan's future is on the agenda. Your own data shows the recommendations are accurate: members flagged for uncontrolled blood pressure were right 83% of the time. You have 15 minutes.

**Leadership test:** Workflow redesign, incentives, capacity, shared ownership, adoption measurement.

## E4 Model underperforms for a subgroup
*Applies to:* Any team (cross-cutting) · *Time box:* 12 minutes

> A quality analyst notices that dual-eligible members are 29% of the at-risk list but 9% of completed outreach. The model was trained on core-book data. The compliance officer asks whether this is a Health Equity Index exposure. You have 12 minutes.

**Leadership test:** Bias detection, subgroup reporting, validation independence, equity reward exposure.

## Response rubric (0 to 3 per row, 18 maximum)
| Criterion | What a good answer contains | Scoring |
|---|---|---|
| Stop or continue decision | Decides within the time box whether to pause the capability, with a stated reason | 0 none · 1 late or unclear · 2 decided · 3 decided with criteria for restart |
| Owner | Names one accountable executive and an operational lead | 0 none · 1 committee · 2 named · 3 named with authority to act |
| Investigation | Defines what to verify first and how (scope, data, timeline) | 0 none · 1 vague · 2 scoped · 3 scoped with hypotheses |
| Stakeholder communication | Members, providers, regulator, board, press: who hears what, when, from whom | 0 none · 1 internal only · 2 external plan · 3 sequenced, honest, member-first |
| Member and provider protection | Concrete steps to limit harm (callbacks, corrections, refunds, provider notice) | 0 none · 1 generic · 2 specific · 3 specific and resourced |
| Corrective action | Durable remediation: control, validation, process change, not just an apology | 0 none · 1 cosmetic · 2 control added · 3 control added and monitored |
