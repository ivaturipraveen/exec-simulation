## Commentary

- **Green:** Both Part D adherence measures hold at 4 Stars at the enterprise level for the third straight cycle. The pharmacy committee describes adherence as "stable and at target."
- **Red:** Two triple-weighted or double-weighted measures now sit at 2 Stars. Readmissions moved from 3 Stars to 2 Stars in 2026 and kept worsening. Appeals fell from 3 Stars to 2 Stars after the 2025 upheld rate dropped to 84.0.
- **Watch:** Complaints rose from 0.27 to 0.30 per 1,000. That is still 3 Stars, but the gap to the 0.50 two-Star threshold has narrowed.
- **Board mandate:** Return the core contract to 4.0, and so protect its quality bonus, and lift Lakeshore to 3.5 within two rating cycles.
