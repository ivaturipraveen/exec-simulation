## Basis

To compare investments, we use one illustrative figure: **$600 per member per year** for a contract at 4.0 Stars or higher. It covers the quality bonus payment and its effect on rebates and benefit competitiveness. It is an order-of-magnitude planning number, not a payment calculation. Real quality bonus payments depend on county benchmarks, bids and rebate share.

Payment timing: a contract's Star rating released in October of year N sets its bonus status for payment year N+1. The 2027 ratings (released October 2026) set payment year 2028.

## Sensitivity

- Each tenth of a point of core summary score near the 3.75 rounding threshold is worth up to $160M a year.
- For the core summary score, one triple-weighted measure moving up one star adds roughly 0.06 to 0.08 points. One double-weighted measure adds roughly 0.04 to 0.05.
- On the 2027 preview the core contract scored **3.71**, which is **0.04** below the threshold that rounds to 4.0.

## Notes for transformation teams

- Compare two-year investment costs with the annual bonus value at stake, using the normalized benefit in the game scorecard (benefit divided by bonus value).
- Measures lag: performance in a measurement year shows up in the rating released about 21 months later, and in bonus payments the year after that. Every payment year at 3.5 costs the core contract about $160M.
- Revenue: $5.9B. The core bonus alone is about 2.7% of revenue.
