# 4-Star Bonus Value Estimate (Illustrative)

**From:** Marcus Lindqvist, CFO
**To:** Dana Whitcomb, President and CEO; plan executive team
**Date:** 19 August 2026
**Re:** What crossing 4.0 Stars is worth, for board and capital planning

## Bottom line

**Crossing from 3.5 to 4.0 Stars is worth about $57M a year (illustrative)** at current membership. That is about 4.4% of revenue, and about three times the plan's forecast 2026 operating margin.

## Calculation

| Input | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Members | 94,800 | Enrollment, August 2026 |
| Illustrative value of a 4-Star rating, per member per year | $600 | Planning assumption (quality bonus payment plus rebate effect) |
| **Annual value** | **94,800 x $600 = $56.9M, about $57M** | |
| Revenue (illustrative) | $1.3B | Finance |
| 2026 operating margin forecast | 1.4%, about $18M | Finance |

The $600 figure is a planning assumption that combines the quality bonus payment on the benchmark with the higher rebate share that a 4-Star plan keeps. It is not a CMS payment calculation. Actual value depends on county benchmarks, the bid and how rebates are used.

## Timing

| Event | Timing |
|---|---|
| Measurement year | Calendar 2027 for most HEDIS and Part D measures; spring 2028 CAHPS |
| Star Ratings published | October 2028 (2029 Star Ratings) |
| Bonus affects payment | Contract year 2029 |

Improvements made in 2027 are not reflected in payments until 2029. Leading indicators are the only evidence the board will see in the meantime.

## Context for decisions

- **A rating drop would cost more than a flat rating.** Falling to 3.0 Stars would reduce the rebate share further, by about $9M a year by our estimate, and would weaken the case to the system board.
- Overall rating movement depends on weighted measures. Outcome and intermediate outcome measures are weighted 3, patient experience and complaints 2, and process measures 1 (as understood for the 2026 Star Ratings).
- The full 4-Star value is not earned by "nearly 4": the overall rating must round to 4.0.

## Caveat

All figures are illustrative and should not be used in a bid or external filing without actuarial review (Hollis and Grant Actuarial). Please cite this memo as "illustrative" in all board materials.
