¹ Abandonment counts callers who hang up after reaching an agent queue. Under VoxBridge platform configuration VB-IVR-07, callers held longer than 4 minutes in the IVR (interactive voice response) pre-queue are disconnected with a "please call back later" message. These disconnects are logged as "IVR completed" and are counted neither as calls offered nor as abandoned. VoxBridge logged 4,180 IVR-completed disconnects in Q2. The setting dates from the 2023 implementation and has not been reviewed.
