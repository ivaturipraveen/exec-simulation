# PBM Refill Reminder Program: Semiannual Summary

**From:** Account Management, ClearRx Pharmacy Benefit Services (pharmacy benefit manager)
**To:** Dr. Samuel Osei, Director of Pharmacy, Horizon Health
**Date:** 18 July 2026
**Period:** 1 January to 30 June 2026
**Program:** ClearRx RefillReady (standard Medicare tier), included in the administrative fee

## Program design

RefillReady sends a reminder when a member is 7 days from running out of a maintenance medication in the three Part D adherence classes (diabetes medications, RAS antagonists, statins). The channel is chosen from the member's contact profile in this order: SMS, then mail. Interactive voice calls are available at an extra fee and are not in Horizon's contract. Contact data comes from Horizon's monthly eligibility file.

## ClearRx observations

1. SMS is the default because it is the cheapest channel, but its delivery rate on Horizon's book is the lowest of our Medicare clients (median 74%).
2. Members in the new counties get SMS-first reminders at a higher rate even though their phone data is less reliable.
3. A "mail-first for members 75 and older" rule can be switched on at no cost. It needs a written instruction from Horizon. No instruction has been received.
4. We can add pharmacist outbound calls through ClearRx Clinical Services at $4.10 per completed call.

ClearRx counts a reminder as "reached" when it is sent. Horizon's internal outreach dashboard reports the program at 100% reach.
