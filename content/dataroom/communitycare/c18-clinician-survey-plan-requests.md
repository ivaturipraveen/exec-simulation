# Clinician Survey on Plan Requests, 2026

**Source:** Provider Engagement team, CommunityCare Alliance, with the Cascade Valley Health Primary Care Leadership Council
**Period:** Fielded 16 June to 18 July 2026 (online, 14 questions and free text)
**Owner:** Monique Adeyemi, Manager, Provider Engagement
**Distribution:** Quality Committee; Joint Plan-System Council

## Method and response

- Census of all 300 PCPs attributed to plan members: 210 employed by Cascade Valley Health (CVH) and 90 independent.
- **n = 188 responded (62.7%)**: employed 141 of 210 (67%), independent 47 of 90 (52%).
- Responses are unweighted. Percentages are of respondents.

## Results

### How clinicians view plan requests

| Statement | Employed | Independent | All |
|---|---|---|---|
| Describe plan gap lists as "administrative burden" | 76% | 57% | **71%** |
| Gap recommendations are clinically reasonable, when seen | 82% | 79% | 81% |
| Plan priorities are part of how my performance is judged | 4% | 49% | 15% |
| Know who at the plan to call about a recommendation | 18% | 62% | 29% |

### Use of the GapConnect portal

| Item | Employed | Independent | All |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Use the portal at least monthly** | 6% | 19% | **9%** |
| Have never logged in | 58% | 21% | 49% |
| Remember their portal password | 22% | 55% | 30% |
| A medical assistant or nurse downloads the PDF for me | 27% | 34% | 29% |

### Where clinicians would act on a recommendation (choose up to 2)

| Location | All |
|---|---|
| In the EHR during the visit (chart banner or order suggestion) | 74% |
| In the EHR inbox, routed to my pool | 61% |
| From my care manager or medical assistant | 38% |
| Plan portal | 4% |
| Fax or mailed list | 2% |

### Reasons for not acting on plan requests (multi-select)

| Reason | All |
|---|---|
| No time in the visit; non-visit work is not recognized | 58% |
| Patient has no upcoming visit, and none is available to book | 51% |
| Did not see it | 47% |
| Data was already out of date | 21% |
| Disagree clinically | 5% |

## Verbatims

> "I've never seen a plan recommendation in the chart. If it isn't in the inbox, it doesn't exist." (employed PCP, Cascade)

> "We're one family on the org chart. In my day, the plan is just another payer asking for things." (employed PCP, Bellmont)

> "I like the lists. The plan pays me to work them." (independent PCP, Orchard)

> "My schedule is booked five weeks out. Telling me a patient needs a BP recheck doesn't create a slot." (employed PCP, Granite)

> "Nobody has ever told me the plan's Star Rating, or why it should matter to me." (employed NP, Cascade)

> "Another login, another password, another list. No." (employed PCP, Cascade)

## Survey team notes

- 71% of all respondents call plan gap lists "administrative burden". Only 9% use the portal monthly.
- Some leaders read the results as a training and awareness issue. The 2025 GapConnect training reached 61% of employed PCPs.
