# Transitions of Care Audit

**Source:** Clinical Quality Audit team, review of CareFlow records and claims
**Period:** Index discharges January 1 to March 31, 2026
**Owner:** Grace Mbeki, RN, VP Care Management; audit lead Dr. Omar Siddiqui, Medical Director, Quality
**Report date:** May 29, 2026

## Scope and method

- Random sample of 400 inpatient discharges to home or home health, stratified by book: 200 core, 120 Lakeshore and 80 Pinecrest
- Each case reviewed against the Heritage transitions-of-care standard: a care manager contact within 48 hours, medication reconciliation within 7 days, and a primary care follow-up visit within 7 days
- Timestamps taken from CareFlow task creation, care manager notes and the claims paid date

## Results against standard

| Standard | Target | Achieved (weighted to enterprise) | Core | Lakeshore | Pinecrest |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Care manager contact within 48 hours | 75% | **23%** | 35% | 4% | 17% |
| Medication reconciliation within 7 days | 70% | 31% | 42% | 12% | 26% |
| Primary care follow-up visit within 7 days | 60% | 44% | 51% | 31% | 40% |
| 30-day readmission (sample) | n/a | 12.3% | 11.0% | 14.2% | 12.5% |

## Timing findings

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Median days from discharge to CareFlow discharge task created | 6 days (core 1 day; Lakeshore 9 days; Pinecrest 8 days) |
| Cases where the task was created after day 7 | 58% |
| Cases where the member was contacted within 24 hours of the task being created | 81% |
| Average care manager caseload | 142 members (standard: 120) |

## Case observations

1. **Case 0412 (Lakeshore, age 82, heart failure):** The CareFlow task was created on day 10 after discharge. The member was readmitted on day 8 with fluid overload, and the new diuretic dose had never been reconciled.
2. **Case 0087 (core, age 76, pneumonia):** The task was created 6 hours after discharge, with a call the same day and med reconciliation on day 3. No readmission.
3. **Case 0233 (Pinecrest, age 79, hip fracture):** The discharge was first identified from a home health claim on day 12.
4. **Case 0301 (Lakeshore, age 71, diabetes):** Care managers did not know about the admission. The member's daughter called member services on day 5 and was transferred three times.

## Auditor conclusions

- Once care managers know about a discharge, they act quickly: 81% of members are contacted within 24 hours.
- Most failed cases had a discharge task that opened late. Few were failures of care manager effort or skill.
- Caseloads are above standard. Adding care managers without changing the timing pattern would give little benefit.
- Hospitals in the Lakeshore and Pinecrest areas do not notify Heritage of discharges (see ADT feed coverage).
