## Commentary

Engagement keeps rising. App users have better adherence and screening rates and call us half as often. The product team proposes extending push-first outreach to every member journey in 2027 (screenings, HRAs, refills, annual wellness visits). Pulsewise estimates that a 10-point rise in MAU would lift gap closure by 3 to 5 points. That estimate has not been tested on Horizon's data.
