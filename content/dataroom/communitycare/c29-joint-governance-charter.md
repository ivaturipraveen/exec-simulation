# Joint Governance Charter: CommunityCare Alliance and Cascade Valley Health

**Parties:** CommunityCare Alliance, Inc. ("the Plan") and Cascade Valley Health ("the System")
**Effective date:** 1 January 2024
**Term:** Three years, renewing automatically unless either party gives 90 days' notice
**Signed by:** Dana Whitcomb, President and CEO (Plan); Robert Hale, President and CEO (System)
**Reviewed:** 12 August 2026 by Plan Legal (annotation at end)

## Article 1. Purpose

1.1 The Plan and the System share a commitment to high-quality, affordable care for Medicare beneficiaries in the Cascade Valley region.
1.2 This Charter sets out how the parties will work together to achieve shared goals for quality, member experience and total cost of care.

## Article 2. Shared goals

2.1 The parties share the following goals:
(a) achieve and sustain an overall Star Rating of **4.0 or higher**;
(b) improve member access to timely primary care;
(c) reduce avoidable admissions and readmissions;
(d) demonstrate the value of an integrated plan-provider model to the System Board.
2.2 The parties will review progress against these goals each year.

## Article 3. Joint Plan-System Council

3.1 The parties establish a Joint Plan-System Council ("the Council").
3.2 Membership: the Plan CEO, CMO and VP Stars and Quality; the System Chief Operating Officer, VP Primary Care and CMIO. The chair rotates annually.
3.3 The Council meets quarterly. Additional meetings may be called by mutual agreement.
3.4 **The Council will reach decisions by consensus.**

## Article 4. Collaboration

4.1 Each party will designate liaisons for clinical quality, information technology and care management.
4.2 The System will consider Plan requests for clinical workflow, information technology and staffing changes through its standard governance processes.
4.3 The Plan will share quality performance reports with System leadership quarterly.
4.4 Care management staff employed by the System and assigned to Plan members remain System employees and are managed under System policies.

## Article 5. Information sharing

5.1 The parties will share data as permitted under their data use agreement dated 15 November 2023 and applicable law, including HIPAA.
5.2 Each party remains responsible for its own regulatory obligations, including CMS requirements for the Plan.

## Article 6. Resources

6.1 Each party bears its own costs unless otherwise agreed in writing.
6.2 Nothing in this Charter commits either party to capital or operating expenditure.

## Article 7. Disagreements

7.1 The parties will work together in good faith to resolve any disagreement arising under this Charter.

## Article 8. General

8.1 This Charter is a statement of intent. It does not create legally binding obligations, except Article 5.
8.2 Amendments require written agreement of both CEOs.

## Plan Legal annotation, 12 August 2026

- The Charter names shared goals but assigns **no decision rights** over clinic workflow, EHR configuration, clinician compensation or care manager priorities. Those matters stay with System committees under Articles 4.2 and 4.4.
- Article 7 provides **no escalation path** beyond good-faith discussion. No matter has ever been escalated to the System Board.
- Since 2024, 6 Council agenda items have been carried forward three or more times without a decision. Examples are EHR delivery of plan recommendations and care manager reporting.
- The Charter renews on 1 January 2027 unless amended.
