# Adherence Program Summary, Year to Date 2026

**From:** Dr. Samuel Okafor, PharmD, Director of Pharmacy
**To:** Dr. Priya Raman, Chief Medical Officer; Elena Ortiz, VP Stars and Quality
**Date:** 14 August 2026
**Re:** Part D adherence (M1, M2): status, capacity and 2027 asks

## Where we are

- **Diabetes medication adherence (M1) is 85.0%** of members with a proportion of days covered (PDC) of 80% or higher, and **RAS antagonist adherence (M2) is 87.0%**. Both are 3 Stars and both sit 1.0 point below the 4-Star line. The 2026 year-to-date run rate, from the ClearPath PBM (pharmacy benefit manager) weekly file, is 85.2% and 87.1%.
- The adherence denominators are 14,860 members on diabetes medications and 31,420 on RAS antagonists.
- The 90-day fill rate is 57% and mail-order share is 19% (see C-10). The average refill gap is 10.8 days.

## What the program does

1. **Automated refill reminders** (IVR, SMS and mail) go to every member with a refill gap of 5 days or more. They reach 88% of targets but convert 12% (C-11).
2. **Pharmacist outreach calls** go to members the at-risk model flags (prior-year PDC under 85%, new starts, or 2 or more late fills). The pharmacists also do 90-day conversions, cost-share counseling and prescriber outreach.

The at-risk list is not the constraint. Four in ten flagged members get a conversation, and the rest receive reminders only.

## What members tell us

Pharmacists log the main barrier on each completed call (n = 9,840 calls, January to July 2026):

| Barrier | Share of calls |
|---|---|
| Forgot or ran out | 24% |
| Cost or copay | 18% |
| Needs a prescription renewal; no PCP appointment available | 17% |
| **Transportation or distance to the pharmacy** | 14% overall; **37% among rural edge members** |
| Side effects or doubts about the medication | 12% |
| Other | 15% |

Two rural ZIP codes (98794 Granite Upper and 98797 Orchard Hills) have no retail pharmacy within 15 miles. Members there are 2.3 times as likely as metro members to have a refill gap of more than 30 days.

## 2027 asks

- Add **3 pharmacist FTE** ($540,000 a year) to raise at-risk reach toward 60%.
- Fund a **90-day and mail-order conversion drive** with home delivery for the rural edge ZIPs (estimate $320,000).
- Add a **renewal pathway**, such as pharmacist protocol refills or nurse practitioner (NP) telehealth renewals, so that adherence does not depend on PCP slots.

The analytics team has proposed upgrading the at-risk model with shared EHR lab data. We support that, but note that the current model already finds more at-risk members than we can call.
