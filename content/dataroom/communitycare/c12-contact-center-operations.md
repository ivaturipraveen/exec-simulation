## Commentary

- **Member Services continues to perform at a 4-Star level.** CSAT has held at 4.4 or above for five of the last six quarters, and FCR reached 78%, its highest level since 2023. The 2025 CAHPS Customer Service composite was 91 (4 Stars).
- Abandonment returned to 6.0% after the annual enrollment period peak. A schedule change (adding a 4 to 8 p.m. shift) is planned for AEP 2026.
- The "finding a doctor or getting an appointment" category is growing. Agents can see clinic availability in a read-only scheduling view but cannot book appointments. They warm-transfer members to the clinic call center, and 31% of those transfers are not completed. Calls closed with a transfer count as resolved for FCR purposes.
- Team leads report that the most common reason for a supervisor escalation is "member upset about wait for appointment" (38% of escalations), ahead of claims disputes (22%).
