# CAHPS Results and Member Verbatims, 2026 Survey

**Source:** 2026 Medicare Advantage and Prescription Drug Plan CAHPS (Consumer Assessment of Healthcare Providers and Systems) survey, administered by Northgate Survey Research (CMS-approved vendor), plus Horizon's optional supplemental open-text module
**Period:** Fielded March to June 2026; covers members' experience in the prior six months
**Owner:** Carla Brandt, Vice President, Member Experience

## Method

- Sample: 2,400 members drawn by CMS protocol; mail with telephone follow-up in English and Spanish.
- Completed surveys: **n = 813** (33.9% response rate). Legacy counties 512, new counties 301.
- Customer service composite base: 774 respondents who reported contacting the plan.
- Supplemental open-text item: "Is there anything else you want to tell us about your health plan?" 286 comments, coded by two analysts.
- Scores are case-mix adjusted composite scores on a 0 to 100 scale. Star levels use the game thresholds.

## Verbatims (lightly edited for length)

## Analyst note

Customer service courtesy is strong. The decline comes from "getting the help you needed," and the open-text responses point to reaching an agent at all. Among respondents who said they could not get through, 62% live in new counties.
