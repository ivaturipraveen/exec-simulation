# Capital Request and System Board Memo

**From:** Marcus Lindqvist, CFO, CommunityCare Alliance, on behalf of Dana Whitcomb, President and CEO
**To:** Board of Directors, Cascade Valley Health; Finance and Strategy Committee
**Date:** 8 September 2026
**Re:** Approval of the $12.0M plan performance capital envelope and the board's review of plan ownership

## 1. Decision recorded

At its 27 August 2026 meeting, the Finance and Strategy Committee **approved a $12.0M capital and transformation envelope** (Round 1) for CommunityCare Alliance, with conditions:

- Funds are released in tranches against a portfolio plan presented to the full board.
- A **second round of $8.0M base, plus up to $4.0M more**, will be considered after the board reviews progress and the quality of the plan's investment case.
- Every investment is to be compared with the clinical capital uses the system has deferred. Those include the **$14M Bellmont ambulatory surgery expansion** and the **$9M oncology infusion center**.

## 2. The board's question

Several directors have asked whether the system should keep the plan. The Committee chair's summary:

> "The plan was meant to prove that a provider-owned plan can deliver better outcomes at lower cost. After three years at 3.5 Stars, the board needs to see a credible path to 4.0, or we should consider a sale or a joint venture with a national carrier."

An outside adviser (Larchmont Partners) estimated an indicative sale value of $160M to $210M at 3.5 Stars, and higher at 4.0 Stars. **The board will revisit ownership at its 2027 strategy retreat.**
