## Commentary

- **IVR.** The Calloway Voice Systems contract lapsed on January 31, 2026. The replacement request for proposal (RFP) was paused in February pending budget. Calloway now provides break-fix only, so no menu or routing changes can be made. Two 2026 plan-year changes (the new over-the-counter benefit and the Lakeshore pharmacy network change) have no IVR path. Those calls land in the general queue and are transferred.
- **Handle time.** Agents work in up to three CRMs depending on the member's book. Lakeshore calls average 13.4 minutes against 10.5 in core. Agents report searching more than one system to find the right member record.
- **Staffing.** Attrition is running at 31% a year. Each new hire takes 9 weeks to reach full productivity.
- **Cost.** The contact center costs $41M a year. Overtime ran $1.9M in the first half of 2026, against a $0.8M budget.
- **Customer Service (CAHPS).** The survey score is still 87 (see the CAHPS trend). The survey was fielded from March to June 2026 and asked about the previous six months.
