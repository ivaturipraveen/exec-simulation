# Member Access Pulse Survey, Spring 2026

**Source:** CommunityCare Alliance Member Experience team; fielded by Ridgeline Survey Partners
**Period:** Fielded 6 April to 22 May 2026, covering the previous 6 months
**Owner:** Tom Brennan, VP Member Experience
**Distribution:** Plan executive team; Quality Committee

## Method

- Stratified random sample of 6,000 members drawn from all four counties and all products. The rural edge ZIPs were oversampled (2x) and the results reweighted.
- Mixed mode: mail, web and phone follow-up. Questions mirror the CAHPS wording where possible, but this is an internal pulse survey, not the official CAHPS survey.
- **n = 1,840 completed (30.7% response).** By county: Cascade 942, Bellmont 418, Orchard 286, Granite 194. Margin of error ±2.3 points at the plan level.

## Results

### Biggest problem with your health care in the last 6 months (single choice)

| Response | % |
|---|---|
| Waiting too long for an appointment | 34% |
| Cost of care or drugs | 15% |
| Getting to appointments or the pharmacy (distance, transport) | 11% |
| Getting a referral to a specialist | 9% |
| Understanding my benefits | 7% |
| Problems with the plan's customer service | 3% |
| No problems | 21% |

### Wait for the most recent routine appointment with the member's PCP

| Wait | All members | Metro and suburban | Rural edge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Within 1 week | 24% | 26% | 14% |
| 1 to 3 weeks | 38% | 39% | 31% |
| **Over 3 weeks** | **38%** | **35%** | **55%** |
