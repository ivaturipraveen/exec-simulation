# Heritage Medicare — team pack
_Simulated, fictional training material. Stars values are illustrative and not predictive._

**15-year legacy incumbent.** Scale, history and data everywhere, none of it joined up.

## Briefing
Heritage Medicare is a 15-year incumbent with 431,000 members across three books: the Heritage core (268,000) plus the acquired Lakeshore (97,000) and Pinecrest (66,000) books. It has scale, ten years of clean history on the core book, value-based contracts covering 35% of members and a strong 38-person quality team. It also runs 14 source systems, 3 CRMs and 2 claims platforms, and 11% of members cannot be matched confidently across them. The enterprise sits at 3.5 Stars; the Lakeshore contract is at 3.0. You have $15.0M of Round 1 capital from a $25M two-year envelope and 8 capacity points a quarter. A $9M data program was cancelled two years ago with nothing to show, and the board has not forgotten it.

**Board mandate:** Hold 4.0 Stars on the core contract and lift Lakeshore to 3.5 within two cycles. The board has approved a $25M transformation envelope and expects visible progress at each quarterly review. A failed flagship program two years ago makes the board allergic to long foundation builds.

**Population.** Older, more complex, with a high dual-eligible share concentrated in the Lakeshore book. Social barriers (transport, housing instability) and fragmented care across many health systems reduce the lift any single intervention can produce. Longitudinal data is rich for the core book and poor for acquired books.

**Visible advantages**
- Scale: a one-point adherence lift is worth more here than anywhere
- Ten years of clean history on 268,000 core members
- Established value-based contracts covering 35% of members
- Institutional knowledge of Stars operations; strong quality team of 38

**Visible constraints**
- Three member identity schemes; 11% of members cannot be matched confidently across pharmacy, medical and CRM data
- Pharmacy outreach capacity reaches only the top 15% of at-risk members
- Discharge (ADT) feeds cover 2 of 5 major health systems; the rest arrive 9 days late via claims
- Change control cycle is 6 weeks; any system change competes with a core claims platform migration
