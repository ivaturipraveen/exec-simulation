# CAHPS Results and Member Verbatims

**Source:** Medicare Advantage and Prescription Drug Plan CAHPS (Consumer Assessment of Healthcare Providers and Systems) survey, administered by Ridgeline Survey Partners; plan preview reports
**Period:** Spring 2025 survey (used in the 2026 Star Ratings), with preliminary spring 2026 results
**Owner:** Tom Brennan, VP Member Experience; analysis by the Stars and Quality team
**Date:** 26 August 2026

## Method

- CMS-specified random sample of 1,600 enrollees, administered by mail with telephone follow-up.
- Spring 2025 survey: **n = 512 completed (32.0%).** Preliminary spring 2026 survey: n = 498 (31.1%).
- Composite scores are case-mix adjusted, on a 0 to 100 scale. Verbatims come from an optional open-ended question the vendor added to the phone protocol (n = 214 comments).

## Analyst notes

- Customer Service is at 4 Stars and the preliminary 2026 result is the highest the plan has recorded.
- The gap between Customer Service (91.0) and Getting Appointments (72.0) is 19 points. The vendor's book-of-business median gap is 9 points.
- 41% of the open-ended comments mention waiting for or being unable to get an appointment, and 6% mention plan staff negatively.
