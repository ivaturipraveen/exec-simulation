# Appeals Operations Review

**From:** Janet Kowalski, Director, Appeals and Grievances
**To:** Tomas Reyes, Chief Financial Officer; Samuel Adeyemi, Chief Compliance and Privacy Officer
**Date:** June 24, 2026
**Re:** Why our Reviewing Appeals Decisions measure is at 2 Stars and what it would take to fix it

## Background

The Reviewing Appeals Decisions measure (M12) is the share of our Part C reconsideration decisions that the Independent Review Entity (IRE) upholds. In calendar 2025 the IRE upheld **83%** of 1,040 cases we forwarded. That is 2 Stars on our game thresholds, down from 86.1% two cycles ago. Appeals cost **$6M** a year to run, with 58 FTE.

## Two systems, two processes

| | AppealTrack (Heritage core) | CaseBridge (Lakeshore, Pinecrest) |
|---|---|---|
| Cases forwarded to IRE (2025) | 610 | 430 |
| IRE upheld rate | 86.1% | 78.6% |
| Average cycle time, standard pre-service (days) | 19 | 27 |
| Clinical notes stored as | Searchable text | Scanned images |
| Pre-IRE quality review | 55% of cases | 18% of cases |
| Templates for case file | 2024 CMS-aligned | 2019 vintage |

Enterprise average cycle time is 22.3 days. Pre-IRE quality review covers 40% of cases.

## Documentation completeness

We audited all 1,040 IRE case files from 2025:

| Finding | Cases | % of total |
|---|---|---|
| Case file missing at least one required IRE document | **177** | **17%** |
| Missing physician notes or records requested from provider | 81 | 7.8% |
| Missing or incomplete coverage rationale letter | 46 | 4.4% |
| Missing evidence of timely notice to member | 29 | 2.8% |
| Wrong member or plan-year benefit language attached | 21 | 2.0% |

Of the 177 incomplete files, the IRE overturned 92 (52%). Complete files were overturned 10% of the time. Lakeshore and Pinecrest cases make up 64% of the incomplete files.

## Root causes cited by reviewers

- Reviewers must search both systems and the provider portal to put a file together.
- In CaseBridge, scanned records cannot be searched, so missing pages are missed.
- Some Lakeshore cases are filed under a member ID different from the one on the original claim, so prior records are hard to find.
- No overflow capacity when volume spikes. The overflow vendor, Meridian Review Partners, handles only clinical review, not file assembly.

## Options

| Option | One-time cost | Expected effect |
|---|---|---|
| Move Lakeshore and Pinecrest appeals onto AppealTrack | $1.4M, 3 quarters | One process; needs integration capacity during the claims migration |
| Document intelligence: auto-summarize records and check files for completeness before IRE submission | $1.25M over 2 years | Vendor estimate: cycle time down 40%, incomplete files under 5% |
| Add 6 reviewers for 100% pre-IRE quality review | $0.7M a year | Upheld rate +3 to 4 points |

## Recommendation

Fund one option in Round 1. The measure moves in the next rating year at about 70% of full effect, so 2026 decisions drive the 2028 rating.
