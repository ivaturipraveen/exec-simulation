## Queue rules (from the system IT intake policy, rev. 2024)

- Requests are prioritized quarterly by the system IT Steering Committee. Patient safety, regulatory work and hospital operations come first.
- **Any change that touches Meridian EHR clinician workflow (inbox, chart banners, order sets, alerts) requires approval by the system CMIO (Chief Medical Information Officer)** and a Clinical Informatics review before it is scheduled.
- The plan has no seat on the Steering Committee. Its requests are submitted through the system's VP of Payer Strategy.
