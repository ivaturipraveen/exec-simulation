# EHR Inbox Workflow Study: Primary Care

**From:** Dr. Lena Marsh, Chief Medical Information Officer (CMIO), Cascade Valley Health, and the Clinical Informatics team
**To:** Primary Care Leadership Council; copy Dr. Priya Raman, Chief Medical Officer, CommunityCare Alliance
**Date:** 22 May 2026
**Re:** How primary care clinicians handle inbox work in Meridian EHR

## Purpose

The study was commissioned to address clinician burnout. It describes how primary care clinicians spend inbox time and how quickly they act on different item types. The plan asked to be copied because of its interest in clinician workflow.

## Method

- EHR audit-log analysis for all 210 employed PCPs, 1 January to 31 March 2026: 1.62 million inbox items.
- Time-motion observation of 24 clinicians across 8 clinics (96 clinic sessions).
- Structured interviews with 12 clinicians and 8 clinic directors.

## Findings

### 1. Clinicians act on the inbox quickly

| Inbox item type | Share of items | Acted on within 48 hours | Median time to action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lab and imaging results | 34% | 93% | 6 hours |
| Patient portal messages | 22% | 88% | 11 hours |
| Prescription renewal requests | 17% | 91% | 9 hours |
| Staff messages and care manager notes | 12% | 79% | 18 hours |
| Referral and order co-sign requests | 9% | 74% | 22 hours |
| System best-practice alerts routed to the inbox | 6% | 51% | 41 hours |
| **All inbox items** | 100% | **84%** | 10 hours |

### 2. Inbox time is large and mostly after hours

- Median **74 inbox items a day** for each full-time PCP. Inbox time is 1 hour 52 minutes a day, of which 58 minutes falls outside scheduled clinic hours.
- Clinicians triage by sender and item type. Items from known pools (care team, pharmacy, lab) are handled first.

### 3. Items from outside the EHR do not enter the inbox

- Plan GapConnect recommendations, payer faxes and external registry reports are **not routed to the inbox**. **Portal items never reach the inbox.** Plan e-mail notifications go to clinicians' system e-mail accounts, which 71% of observed clinicians check less than once a day.
- During the 96 observed sessions, no clinician opened the plan portal. Medical assistants opened it on 3 occasions, to print PDFs for the morning huddle.

### 4. Ownership of non-visit work is unclear

- 7 of 8 clinic directors said population health work "belongs to the care managers". Care managers interviewed said they take priorities from their clinic director.
- Clinicians said that work arriving with a named owner and an order ready to sign gets done. Work that arrives as a list does not.

## Recommendations (clinical informatics)

1. Reduce low-value best-practice alerts by 30% (in progress, Q3 2026).
2. Before adding any new inbox item type, require CMIO review of volume, routing and ownership.
3. Pilot team-based inbox pools so that nurses handle renewals and results under protocol.

## Note on external requests

Clinical Informatics has received a plan request to route recommendations into the EHR (see the system IT change queue, C-22). Under recommendation 2, that request will be assessed for inbox burden before it is scheduled.
