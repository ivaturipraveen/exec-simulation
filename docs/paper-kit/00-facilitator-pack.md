## Contingency cuts
| Condition | Cut |
|---|---|
| Behind by 10 minutes or more at 1:10 | Cut Analyze to 8 minutes; facilitator reads the key variance aloud instead of free interrogation |
| Behind by 10 minutes or more at 1:58 | Compress the operating model exercise to steps 3 to 6 only (12 minutes) |
| Behind by 10 minutes or more at 2:16 | Run the crisis as a single team-wide event with one shared response (10 minutes) |
| Any time | Drop the cross-team comparison discussion; show the scorecards and move to debrief |
| Technology failure | Paper fallback: printed payer briefs, decision forms, pre-computed results packets for the four most likely portfolios per payer |

## Console actions by stage
- **Executive setup and briefing:** Start session; Assign teams to payers; Start the stage clock
- **Meet your company:** Confirm every team has joined and opened its briefing
- **Diagnose:** Open the data room; Monitor the query log; Nudge teams that have not cited evidence by minute 15
- **Investment Round 1:** Confirm submissions; Ledger shows committed and available; Lock at the timebox
- **Simulate Year 1 (break):** Run engine; Review before release; Release the performance review
- **Analyze:** Release analyze-layer evidence if needed
- **Investment Round 2 and board pitch:** Score pitches on the rubric; Set earned capital; Reopen the ledger; Lock
- **Simulate Year 2:** Run engine; Review before release; Release Year 2 results
- **Agent operating model:** Monitor submissions; Score value; control; adoption and recoverability
- **Crisis:** Accept or override the recommended event and severity (audit note); Start the response timer; Score the rubric
- **Final results:** Release scorecards; Show the dimension profiles side by side
- **Debrief:** Reveal hidden root causes (answer key); Walk the simplification register
- **Real-company translation (optional extension):** Show the aggregate Opportunity Map; Export the map

## Board pitch rubric (15 points) and earned capital
- **Evidence cited:** Round 1 results and at least two data-room artifacts referenced correctly
- **Causal explanation:** Explains why results differed from the thesis in terms of the causal chain
- **Decision quality:** Scales, modifies, pauses or cancels something specific, with a reason
- **Risk acknowledged:** Names at least one risk the portfolio creates and a control for it
- **Clarity under time:** Delivered in 90 seconds; a board member could repeat the ask

| Pitch score | Earned capital added to the base |
|---|---|
| 13+ | +$4M |
| 10+ | +$2M |
| 7+ | +$1M |
| 0+ | +$0M |

## Operating model scoring (0 to 3 each)
- **Value:** Does the design move leading KPIs faster than the status quo?
- **Control adequacy:** Are controls proportionate to consequence and autonomy at each step?
- **Adoption feasibility:** Will the named humans actually do this given incentives and capacity?
- **Recoverability:** Can a wrong action be detected and reversed before member harm?

## Simplification register (say these lines)
| Actual concept | V1 representation | Facilitator disclosure |
|---|---|---|
| Full CMS measure set (40+ measures) | 10 selected measures | “We use 10 of 40-plus measures. The lessons transfer; the arithmetic does not.” |
| Cut points set annually from national distribution | Fixed game thresholds per measure | “Thresholds are game constructs. Real cut points move every year.” |
| Improvement measures (weight 5) | Excluded | “In the real system, improving also pays. We left that out.” |
| Readmissions as a risk-adjusted observed-to-expected ratio | Readmissions per 100 index stays | “Direction and lag are faithful; the statistic is simplified.” |
| CAHPS sampling, case-mix adjustment, survey timing | Composite score with a one-year lag | “Survey results arrive late and reflect the prior six months.” |
| Tukey outlier deletion, guardrails, reward factor mechanics | Omitted; Health Equity Index represented as a subgroup modifier | “We model equity as a modifier; the actual reward mechanics are more detailed.” |
| Contract-level ratings and enrollment weighting | One rating per payer; Heritage shows a Lakeshore sub-rating | “Heritage has one contract that matters for this exercise.” |
| Quality bonus payment and rebate arithmetic | Single illustrative 4-Star bonus value per payer ($600 PMPY) | “The number is illustrative. The cliff is real.” |
| Multi-year investment amortization and operating budgets | Two-year capital envelope plus stated run costs | “Treat the envelope as the board did: one number, two years.” |
| Real PHI and real provider data | Fictional data generated from specified root causes | “Everything in the data room is invented.” |
