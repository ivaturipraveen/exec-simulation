# CAHPS Trend and Verbatims

**Source:** Medicare Advantage and Prescription Drug Plan CAHPS (Consumer Assessment of Healthcare Providers and Systems) survey results through Halden Survey Partners, a CMS-approved vendor
**Period:** 2024, 2025 and 2026 survey administrations (2026 fielded March 3 to June 12, 2026)
**Owner:** Priya Desai, Director, Member Insights
**Distribution:** Executive team, Member Services, Stars Program Office

## Method

- CMS-specified random sample per contract, mail with telephone follow-up
- 2026 enterprise sample: 5,400 members; 2,080 completes (38.5% response). By contract: core 1,150, Lakeshore 520, Pinecrest 410
- Members report on their experience **in the previous 6 months**
- Scores are case-mix adjusted composites on a 0 to 100 scale
